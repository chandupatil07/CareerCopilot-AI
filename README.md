# 🚀 CareerCopilot AI
### AI-Powered Career Assistant for Students & Job Seekers

CareerCopilot AI is an end-to-end AI-powered SaaS platform designed to help students and job seekers manage their complete placement journey from resume building to interview preparation.

It combines Full Stack Development, Machine Learning, NLP, Generative AI, and Agentic AI into one intelligent platform that helps users improve their chances of getting hired.
